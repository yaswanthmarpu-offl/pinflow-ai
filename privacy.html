Privacy Policy

PinFlow AI

Last updated: September 2026

1. About PinFlow AI

PinFlow AI is a personal AI-assisted content management project designed to help the application owner create, organize, and prepare content for Pinterest.

The application is intended for personal use by the application owner and is not currently offered as a service to other users.

2. Information We Access

When PinFlow AI is connected to Pinterest, the application may access information from the authenticated Pinterest account that is necessary to provide the application's functionality.

This may include information related to the authenticated user's Pins and boards, where the relevant Pinterest authorization permits such access.

PinFlow AI does not intentionally request or collect Pinterest login passwords.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the functionality of PinFlow AI, including organizing and preparing Pinterest content for the authenticated account owner.

Pinterest information is not sold or provided to unrelated third parties for their own purposes.

4. AI-Generated Content

PinFlow AI may use artificial intelligence tools to assist with the creation of Pin titles, descriptions, content ideas, and other original content.

Pinterest account data is not intentionally used to train, fine-tune, or develop artificial intelligence models.

5. Affiliate and Product Content

PinFlow AI may assist the application owner in preparing product-related Pinterest content and affiliate links.

Affiliate relationships and destination websites may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies when visiting external websites.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and information used by the application.

Pinterest API credentials and access tokens should be kept private and should not be publicly shared.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

PinFlow AI does not intend to retain Pinterest account data longer than necessary for the functionality of the application.

Pinterest data will be handled and deleted in accordance with applicable Pinterest developer requirements and applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services

PinFlow AI may interact with third-party services such as Pinterest, AI service providers, affiliate platforms, and product websites when required for the application's functionality.

Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of PinFlow AI changes or when necessary to comply with applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or PinFlow AI, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the application.
