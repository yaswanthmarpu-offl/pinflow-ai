About FlowAgent AI

FlowAgent AI is a personal content management tool designed to help create and organize Pinterest content.

The tool assists with preparing Pin images, titles, descriptions, product-related content, and publishing workflows for the account owner.

Features

  • AI-assisted Pinterest content creation
  • Pin title and description generation
  • Product content organization
  • Affiliate content preparation
  • Publishing workflow management
  • Content planning and scheduling assistance

Privacy

FlowAgent AI is designed for personal use. Pinterest account access is provided through Pinterest's authorized authentication mechanisms.

For more information about how information is handled, please read our Privacy Policy.
